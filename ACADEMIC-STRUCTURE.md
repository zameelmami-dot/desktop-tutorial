# Psychology Learning Hub — Academic Structure

## Audience and language
- Primary audience: Undergraduate Psychology students.
- Teaching language: English with Malayalam explanations for accessibility.
- Academic alignment: Use the student's current university syllabus and prescribed readings as the final authority for examination coverage. This hub is a supplementary learning resource.

## Learning design
Every topic should use the same sequence:
1. Learning outcomes — what the student should know or be able to do.
2. Key terms — short definitions in English and Malayalam.
3. Concept explanation — simple, accurate, undergraduate-level notes.
4. Everyday example — familiar examples from student, family and community life.
5. Application — a case, classroom activity or reflection prompt.
6. Knowledge check — MCQs with answer explanations.
7. Revision — summary, key points and short-answer questions.
8. References — textbooks, original sources or credible institutional resources.

## Academic modules

### 1. Foundations of Psychology
- Definition, nature and scope of psychology
- Historical development and schools of thought
- Major branches and career applications
- Psychology as a science; scientific attitude and methods
- Research ethics and introductory research methods

### 2. Basic Psychological Processes
- Learning: classical conditioning, operant conditioning and observational learning
- Memory: encoding, storage, retrieval, memory systems and forgetting
- Attention and perception
- Thinking, problem-solving and intelligence
- Motivation and emotion

### 3. Life Skill Development
- Self-awareness and empathy
- Communication and interpersonal relationships
- Assertiveness and conflict management
- Critical and creative thinking
- Decision-making and problem-solving
- Coping with stress and emotional regulation

### 4. Psychological First Aid
- Meaning, principles and scope of PFA
- Look, Listen and Link
- Safe approach, supportive communication and identifying immediate needs
- Practical assistance, social support and referral
- PFA in emergencies relevant to Kerala
- Helper self-care, boundaries and compassion fatigue

### 5. Research Methods
- Research questions, hypotheses and operational definitions
- Research designs: experimental, correlational, survey and observational
- Variables, population, sample and sampling methods
- Tools and methods of data collection
- Research ethics and informed consent
- Introductory statistics and interpretation

### 6. Practice and Assessment
- Topic-wise MCQs with explanations
- Short-answer and essay questions
- Case-based application questions
- Classroom activities and reflection tasks
- Revision notes and self-assessment

## Development roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation (current)
- Responsive bilingual landing page
- Six academic module cards
- Starter lesson panels
- Topic search and subject filter
- One sample knowledge-check question

### Phase 2 — Lesson library
- Create a separate lesson page or data file for each topic.
- Add English and Malayalam explanations.
- Add learning outcomes, examples, key terms and references.
- Keep the language simple without sacrificing academic accuracy.

### Phase 3 — Assessment
- Build topic-wise MCQ banks.
- Show answer explanations, not only right/wrong feedback.
- Add short-answer practice and revision tests.
- Track quiz score locally only if that feature is later requested.

### Phase 4 — Teaching resources
- Add downloadable class notes and presentation links where permitted.
- Add activities, case studies and assignment prompts.
- Organise content by semester and course after confirming the current syllabus.

## Content quality checklist
- Verify each topic against reliable textbooks or institutional sources.
- Check alignment with the current University/college syllabus before presenting exam guidance.
- Use original explanations; cite sources for definitions, models and research findings.
- Have Malayalam wording reviewed for clarity and correct psychological terminology.
- Distinguish established evidence from examples, opinions and simplified analogies.
- Review accessibility on mobile and desktop.
