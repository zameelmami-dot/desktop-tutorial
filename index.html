🌱 Learning

Meaning: Learning is a relatively lasting change in behaviour or knowledge that occurs through experience or practice.

Everyday example: A student practises riding a bicycle. With practice, balancing and steering become easier. This improvement is an example of learning.

Remember: temporary changes caused only by tiredness, illness or medication are not usually considered learning.

🧩 Memory

Meaning: Memory is the process of retaining information and using it later.

Everyday example: You learn a student's name today and remember it when you meet them next week.

Three basic processes: Encoding (taking in information), storage (retaining it) and retrieval (bringing it back when needed).

💛 Emotional regulation

Meaning: Emotional regulation involves influencing which emotions we have, when we have them, and how we experience or express them.

Everyday example: Before an exam, a student feels anxious and uses slow breathing or encouraging self-talk to manage the feeling.

Two strategies: Cognitive reappraisal changes how we think about a situation; expressive suppression reduces outward expression of an emotion.

🤝 Psychological First Aid

Meaning: Psychological First Aid (PFA) is humane, supportive and practical help for people affected by a serious crisis.

Everyday example: After a flood, a volunteer calmly listens to an affected person, helps them identify immediate needs and connects them with reliable support.

Remember: PFA is not forcing people to talk, diagnosing them or replacing professional mental-health care.
